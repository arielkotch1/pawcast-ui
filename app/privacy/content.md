## Introduction

PawsOut ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and otherwise handle your information when you use our mobile application PawsOut (the "App").

Please read this privacy policy carefully. By using PawsOut, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by all the terms of this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### Location Data
* **Precise Location:** We collect your GPS coordinates to provide dog-friendly weather information and safety calculations specific to your area.
* **How It's Used:** Your location is used to fetch real-time weather data and calculate paw temperature safety estimates. Location data is NOT stored on our servers; it's only used to fetch current weather information.

### Dog Profile Information
When you add a dog to the app, we collect:
* Dog's name
* Dog's photo (stored locally on your device)
* Dog's breed
* Dog's size (Small, Medium, Large)
* Dog's age
* Dog's coat type (Short, Medium, Long)
* Muzzle length

### Preferences
* Temperature unit preference (Celsius/Fahrenheit)
* Time format preference (12h/24h)
* Selected location overrides (if you manually set a location)
* Shade type preferences
* Surface/floor type preferences

### Photo Library Access
We request access to your photo library so you can select a photo of your dog. Photos are stored locally on your device only. We do NOT upload photos to our servers.

### Usage Data
We may collect analytics about how you use the app (feature usage, error reports) through Expo analytics. This helps us improve the app and fix bugs.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

### Primary Purpose
To provide personalized paw temperature safety calculations based on:
* Real-time weather at your location
* Your dog's breed, size, and coat characteristics
* Your selected walking conditions

### Secondary Purposes
* To improve app functionality and user experience
* To fix bugs and provide technical support
* To send important notifications about app updates

**We do NOT:**
* Sell or share your personal data with third parties
* Use your data for marketing or advertising
* Store your location history
* Combine data across multiple users

---

## 3. How Your Data Is Stored

### Local Storage (On Your Device)
All of the following is stored ONLY on your device using local device storage:
* Dog profiles (name, breed, size, age, coat type)
* Dog photos
* Your preferences (units, time format, etc.)
* Your saved locations

*Your data is NOT automatically backed up to the cloud. If you uninstall the app or reset your device, this data will be lost unless you've backed it up separately.*

### Server-Side Storage
We do NOT store personal data on our servers. Your location is only used in real-time to fetch weather data; it is not stored or logged.

### Data Deletion
You can delete all data by:
1. Opening **Settings** in the app
2. Tapping **Delete Data**
3. Confirming the deletion

This permanently removes all dog profiles, photos, and preferences from your device.

## 4. Third-Party Services

PawsOut uses the following third-party services:

### Open-Meteo API
* **Purpose:** Provides weather data and historical temperature information
* **Data Shared:** Your GPS coordinates (latitude/longitude)
* **Privacy Policy:** [Open-Meteo Privacy Policy](https://open-meteo.com/en/privacy)

### Native Device Location Services
* **Purpose:** Provides location identification, geocoding, and map services through your device's native framework (Apple Location Services on iOS / Google Location Services on Android).
* **Data Shared:** Your GPS coordinates
* **Privacy Policy:** [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/privacy/) / [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### Expo
* **Purpose:** App analytics and crash reporting
* **Data Shared:** App usage analytics (non-personal)
* **Privacy Policy:** [Expo Privacy Policy](https://expo.dev/privacy)

## 5. Data Security

We take data security seriously:
* All connections to third-party services use HTTPS encryption.
* Your data is stored locally on your device with the protection mechanisms your device provides.
* We do NOT store sensitive data on remote servers.

*However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we use industry-standard security measures, we cannot guarantee absolute security.*

## 7. Your Rights

* **Delete Your Data:** You can delete all data at any time through the app's **Settings > Delete Data** feature.

## 8. Location Permission Levels

* **"While Using the App":** We access your location only when the app is actively in use. Best for general dog-friendly weather checking.
* **"Always":** We may access your location even when the app is not actively in use. This allows for background weather monitoring and alerts.

You can change this setting at any time in your device's **Settings > Privacy > Location**.
