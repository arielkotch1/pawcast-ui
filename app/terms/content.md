## 10. Emergency Procedures
If your dog shows signs of temperature distress during outdoor activity, **STOP IMMEDIATELY**:

### Heat Distress Signs
Heavy panting, excessive drooling, weakness, glazed eyes, vomiting, or collapse.
* *Action:* Move to shade or air conditioning, offer water, apply cool (not ice-cold) water to their body, and contact a vet immediately.

### Cold Distress Signs
Shivering, whining, lifting paws off the ground, reluctance to walk, or lethargy.
* *Action:* Move indoors immediately, dry paws and body, wrap in warm blankets, and consult a vet.
